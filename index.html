Good to know

Questions,
answered.

What is included in the ticket?

Your pass includes entry to the festival grounds, all scheduled performances, the artist village, and refreshment zones. Pass-specific benefits are listed above.

Is ID required?

Yes. A valid government-issued photo ID matching the name on your ticket is required at entry.

What time does the event start?

Gates open at 4:00 PM on 12 December 2026. We recommend arriving early for a smooth entry.

Are tickets refundable?

Tickets are non-refundable, but you can contact our support team about transfer options up to 72 hours before the event.

Is parking available?

Limited paid parking is available nearby. Public transport and ride-share drop-off are recommended.
